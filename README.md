# About Tanzanite Token  $TZN

Connecting blockchain technology with real-world payment solutions across Africa and beyond.

Tanzanite Token (TZN) is a utility-focused digital asset developed to bridge the gap between traditional finance and blockchain technology. Operating on the BNB Smart Chain, TZN leverages the speed, security, and low transaction costs of BSC to deliver practical payment and reward solutions for individuals and businesses.

The token serves as the native currency of the ChiwawaPay ecosystem — a platform designed to simplify digital commerce, enable cross-border transactions, and empower merchants with blockchain-based tools for payments, loyalty programs, and financial services.

This token can be used as a payment option in different merchants around Tanzania and the world at large
